# IC-7110 rig file: draft

Date: 20261127

`rigs/IC-7110.rig` is a draft for the Icom IC-7110 (announced at the Tokyo Ham Fair
2026), made before the radio is here and before Icom has published a CI-V reference for
it. It is wfview's `IC-705.rig` with the changes below, taken from RS-BA1's own
definition files for the IC-7110 and the IC-705 (its model `.ini` files, compared
function by function). Untested: check each point on the radio.

## From RS-BA1's IC-7110 definition

- CI-V address **0xBA** (186). Connections: USB, Wi-Fi, RS-BA1 remote; no Ethernet.
- RS-BA1 classes it as the IC-705 and IC-9700 (`TYPE` split), not as the IC-785x /
  IC-7760 (two equal receivers). Of RS-BA1's 244 functions it differs from the IC-705
  in 48, from the IC-9700 in 72: the IC-705 is the base.
- Frequency groups: 30 kHz - 74.8 MHz (HF, 6 m, 4 m), 74.8 - 200 MHz (VHF, airband and
  WFM receive included), 400 - 470 MHz. No 23 cm.
- Moved `1A 05` items (IC-705 -> IC-7110):

| Function | IC-705 | IC-7110 |
|---|---|---|
| DATA OFF MOD input | 1A 05 01 18 | 1A 05 01 27 |
| DATA MOD input | 1A 05 01 19 | 1A 05 01 28 |
| USB mod level | 1A 05 01 16 | 1A 05 01 25 (see below) |
| LAN (Wi-Fi) mod level | 1A 05 01 17 | 1A 05 01 26 (see below) |
| CW dash ratio | 1A 05 02 52 | 1A 05 02 94 |
| Overflow | 15 07 | 1A 09 |

- DATA OFF MOD / DATA MOD inputs: 0 MIC, 1 USB, 2 ACC, 3 MIC+USB, 4 MIC+ACC, **5 Wi-Fi**
  (IC-705: Wi-Fi is 3). The app sets DATA OFF MOD to the LAN input at connect: value 5.
- Attenuator 20 dB up to 74.8 MHz, 10 dB on V / UHF; preamp off, 1, 2.
- Po meter: the same raw points as the IC-705 and IC-9700, on the 100 W scale (RS-BA1
  `PO_TYPE` 0, as the 9700; the 705 is the 10 W scale): the IC-7300's Po table.
- One scope, spans 2.5 - 500 kHz, as the IC-705. Power on / off 18 01 / 18 00.
- Extra over the IC-705 (not in the draft yet): APF, VSC, NB depth and width per band
  group (HF, V, U), the ACC input and its level, band edge commands. Missing against the
  IC-705: its battery / DC power limits.

## Inferred, not stated

- **Which mod level is which.** RS-BA1 numbers the levels Mod1 / Mod2 / Mod3 without
  naming the input. On the IC-9700 they follow the input order ACC, USB, LAN; on the
  IC-705 (no ACC) USB, LAN. So on the IC-7110: Mod1 = ACC (01 24), Mod2 = USB (01 25),
  Mod3 = Wi-Fi (01 26).
- **Band power**: 100 W on HF and 6 m, 50 W on 4 m (European version), 2 m and 70 cm,
  from the announcement. AM a quarter of that (the app and x6100_trx assume it).
- **4 m**: 70.0 - 70.5 MHz, region 1, no band stacking register code (`BSR=0`: not known).

## Switched off (no get, no set)

`1A 05` items the IC-705 file has but RS-BA1 gives no IC-7110 number for: Quick Split,
REF Adjust, CI-V Transceive, System Date, System Time, UTC Offset. Left at the IC-705's
numbers they would change some other menu setting on the IC-7110.

## Not in the draft: to see on the radio

- **The second receiver.** The draft has one receiver with VFO A / B, as the IC-705:
  that works on a rig that answers the IC-705's commands. How the IC-7110 does main /
  sub and dual watch over CI-V (07 D0 / D1 as the IC-9700? Selected / Unselected Freq?
  Command29?) is not in RS-BA1's files (it shows no main / sub for the IC-9700 either).
- The band stacking register codes (1A 01), for 4 m in particular.
- The scope's data layout (the IC-705's, presumably).
- The extras above, and the switched-off items, once Icom's CI-V reference is out.

## First test on the radio

1. CI-V address 0xBA in the rig's menu; the app finds the rig file by it.
2. Frequency, mode, filter, both ways; S-meter; scope.
3. DATA OFF MOD to Wi-Fi at connect (the app; the rig's menu shows it), TX audio, and
   back to the old input at disconnect.
4. Po and the PWR watts on 40 m, 6 m, 4 m, 2 m, 70 cm; AM.
5. Attenuator 20 dB on HF, 10 dB on V / UHF.
6. With the second receiver in use: what the app shows, and what the rig answers to
   07 D0 / D1 and to Unselected Freq (25 01).
